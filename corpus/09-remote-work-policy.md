# Remote Work Policy

**Document ID:** TBP-POL-RW-001
**Version:** 1.4
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026
**Policy Owner:** Chief People Officer
**Approver:** Executive Committee

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the conditions under which ThaBytePay employees may
work outside Group premises, including hybrid and fully remote arrangements.
It balances flexibility with productivity, collaboration, and information
security.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all permanent and contract employees of ThaBytePay
whose roles permit remote work. It does not apply to roles that require
physical presence (branch staff, ATM technicians, field services, security
guards, drivers).

## 3. Definitions

- **Remote Work:** Performing job duties from a location other than a Group
  office, typically the employee's home.
- **Hybrid Schedule:** A mix of office and remote work days each week.
- **Fully Remote:** Working from outside any Group office on a permanent
  basis.
- **Approved Remote Location:** A location declared by the employee, agreed
  by the manager, and consistent with this policy.

## 4. Policy Statements

### 4.1 Eligibility

Eligibility is determined by:

- **Role suitability:** The role's tasks can be performed without physical
  presence.
- **Performance:** Employees must be confirmed past probation and meeting
  performance expectations.
- **Manager approval:** Line managers must agree to the arrangement.
- **Equipment:** The employee must have access to suitable equipment and
  connectivity.

### 4.2 Work Arrangements

The Group supports three patterns:

| Pattern | Description | Approval |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| In-office | Default; 5 days/week on Group premises | N/A |
| Hybrid (2-3) | 2-3 office days per week, remainder remote | Line manager |
| Fully remote | Remote on a permanent basis | Line manager + Department Head |

Cross-border remote work (working outside Kenya) requires HR, Tax, and CISO
approval and may be subject to additional restrictions.

### 4.3 Equipment and Connectivity

- The Group provides a laptop and required peripherals.
- A monthly home-office allowance of 5,000 KES is paid to employees on
  hybrid or fully remote arrangements to offset connectivity and utility
  costs.
- Employees must have a stable broadband connection (minimum 10 Mbps down /
  2 Mbps up).
- Public Wi-Fi may not be used for work without VPN.

### 4.4 Workspace and Wellbeing

- Employees should maintain a dedicated, ergonomic workspace.
- The Group's wellbeing programme covers all employees regardless of work
  location.
- Working hours are 09:00 to 17:00 local time unless otherwise agreed.
  Employees should disconnect outside working hours.
- Meeting-free Fridays apply across the Group to reduce video fatigue.

### 4.5 Security Requirements

When working remotely, employees must:

- Use the Group-issued laptop with the EDR agent active (per Information
  Security Policy)
- Connect via the Group VPN when accessing internal systems
- Lock the laptop when stepping away
- Ensure conversations involving Confidential or Restricted data are not
  overheard
- Not allow family members or others to use the work laptop
- Maintain MFA on all corporate accounts

### 4.6 Confidentiality

- Printed materials with Confidential or Restricted information must be
  shredded or returned to the office for disposal.
- Customer calls must be taken in a private setting where conversation
  cannot be overheard.
- No screenshots of customer data may be saved to personal cloud accounts.

### 4.7 Collaboration Expectations

- Employees on hybrid arrangements should align in-office days with their
  team's anchor days (typically Tuesday and Wednesday).
- All employees, regardless of work location, must be available during
  business hours for meetings, on-call duties (where applicable), and team
  communications.
- Cameras on during team meetings is encouraged but not mandatory.

### 4.8 Performance Management

- Performance management is identical for in-office and remote employees.
- Output, deliverables, and behaviours are evaluated; presence is not a
  performance metric.
- Managers conduct one-on-ones with remote staff at least weekly.

### 4.9 Termination of Arrangement

The remote work arrangement may be terminated:

- At the employee's request (with reasonable notice)
- By the manager for performance, security, or business reasons (with
  written notice of at least 14 days)
- Automatically if the employee's role changes to one requiring physical
  presence

Upon termination of employment, all Group equipment must be returned within
5 business days.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Chief People Officer:** Owns this policy.
- **Line Managers:** Approve arrangements and monitor performance and
  engagement.
- **HR Business Partners:** Advise on policy application.
- **IT and Information Security:** Equipment provisioning, secure access,
  device management.
- **Employees:** Comply with this policy and maintain security and
  professional standards.

## 6. Compliance & Enforcement

Violations of remote work security requirements are treated as breaches of
the Information Security Policy and may result in disciplinary action or
termination of the arrangement.
