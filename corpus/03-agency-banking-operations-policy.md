# Agency Banking Operations Policy

**Document ID:** TBP-POL-AB-001
**Version:** 3.1
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026
**Policy Owner:** Head of Agency Banking
**Approver:** Board Risk & Compliance Committee

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the operation of ThaBytePay's Agency Banking channel,
which extends financial services through a network of authorised non-bank
agents. It defines agent onboarding, transaction limits, cash management,
liquidity requirements, and ongoing supervision.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All ThaBytePay employees involved in agent recruitment, onboarding,
  monitoring, or support.
- All authorised agents operating under the Group's Agency Banking licence.
- All systems used to process agent-initiated transactions, including the
  Agent Banking Platform (ABP), USSD gateway, agent mobile application, and
  switch integrations.

## 3. Definitions

- **Agent:** A non-bank entity authorised by the Group and approved by the
  regulator to provide specified financial services on the Group's behalf.
- **Master Agent:** An agent authorised to recruit and supervise sub-agents
  within a defined geographic territory.
- **Float:** The pre-funded balance an agent maintains to settle customer
  transactions in real time.
- **Cash-In/Cash-Out (CICO):** Deposit and withdrawal transactions performed
  through an agent.
- **Tier:** A classification of agents based on transaction volume, risk
  profile, and operational maturity (see Section 4.3).

## 4. Policy Statements

### 4.1 Agent Onboarding

All prospective agents must complete:

- Submission of a completed Agent Application Form with supporting documents
  (business registration, KRA PIN certificate, proof of physical premises,
  director/owner ID, and bank statement covering the prior 6 months).
- **KYC verification** of all directors and beneficial owners per Section 4
  of the KYC & AML Compliance Policy.
- Physical inspection of the proposed agent outlet by the Agency Field Team.
- Credit and reputational background check including sanctions screening.
- Successful completion of the Agent Onboarding Training Programme
  (minimum 8 hours, in-person or virtual).
- Posting of a security deposit, refundable on termination, in accordance
  with Section 4.6.

Onboarding decisions must be made within 14 business days of application
receipt. Rejected applicants must be notified in writing with reasons.

### 4.2 Permitted Transactions

Authorised agents may perform the following on behalf of the Group:

- Account opening (Tier-1 KYC accounts only)
- Cash deposits to customer accounts
- Cash withdrawals from customer accounts
- Inter-account transfers within the Group
- Bill payments to onboarded billers
- Mobile money cash-in and cash-out (where partner integrations exist)
- Customer card activation (per Section 5 of the Card Issuance & Management
  Policy)

Agents are **strictly prohibited** from:

- Lending or extending credit in any form
- Accepting foreign currency transactions
- Performing transactions outside agreed business hours without authorisation
- Charging fees beyond the published Group tariff
- Subcontracting services to unauthorised parties

### 4.3 Agent Tiers and Transaction Limits

Daily transaction limits are determined by agent tier:

| Tier | Daily Volume (KES) | Single Transaction Limit (KES) | Float Minimum (KES) |
|------|--------------------|--------------------------------|---------------------|
| Tier-1 | 500,000 | 35,000 | 50,000 |
| Tier-2 | 2,000,000 | 70,000 | 200,000 |
| Tier-3 | 5,000,000 | 150,000 | 500,000 |
| Master Agent | 15,000,000 | 250,000 | 1,500,000 |

Tier upgrades are reviewed quarterly based on transaction history, compliance
record, and float discipline. Upgrades require approval by the Head of Agency
Banking.

### 4.4 Float and Liquidity Management

- Agents must maintain float at or above the minimum for their tier at all
  times during business hours.
- Float positions are monitored in real time by the Agency Operations team.
  Float drops below 25% of tier minimum trigger an automated low-float alert
  to the agent.
- Agents must reconcile float and cash daily and submit a daily settlement
  report through the Agent Mobile Application by 18:00 local time.
- Float top-ups can be made via:
  - Group teller deposit at any branch
  - Inter-bank transfer to the agent's settlement account
  - Master Agent allocation (for sub-agents)

### 4.5 Commissions and Settlement

- Commissions are calculated on a per-transaction basis per the published
  Commission Schedule, which is updated annually.
- Commissions accrue in real time and are settled daily to the agent's
  designated settlement account no later than 09:00 the following business day.
- Disputes regarding commission calculations must be raised within 30 days
  of the transaction date through the Agency Service Desk.

### 4.6 Security Deposit

- All agents must post a security deposit upon onboarding, ranging from
  100,000 KES (Tier-1) to 2,000,000 KES (Master Agent).
- The deposit is held in a non-interest-bearing escrow account.
- The deposit may be applied against confirmed losses arising from agent
  fraud, theft, or breach of this policy.
- On termination of the agency relationship, the deposit is refunded within
  60 days, net of any deductions for losses or unsettled obligations.

### 4.7 Monitoring and Supervision

- All agent transactions are subject to real-time fraud monitoring per the
  Group's Transaction Monitoring System.
- Monthly compliance scorecards are issued to each agent, covering KYC
  completeness, transaction reversal rates, customer complaints, and float
  discipline.
- Field visits to each agent outlet are conducted at least quarterly by the
  Agency Field Team.
- Master Agents are responsible for monthly supervision of their sub-agents
  and must submit supervision reports to the Group by the 5th of each month.

### 4.8 Termination

The agency relationship may be terminated for cause, including but not
limited to:

- Material breach of this policy or the Agency Agreement
- Fraud, theft, or other criminal activity
- Persistent failure to meet float or service standards
- Adverse change in business circumstances (insolvency, loss of premises)
- Regulatory direction

Termination procedures, notice periods, and offboarding requirements are
detailed in the Agency Agreement and the Termination & Offboarding Standard
Operating Procedure (TBP-SOP-AB-014).

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Head of Agency Banking:** Owns this policy and the overall channel
  performance and risk.
- **Agency Operations Team:** Day-to-day monitoring of agent activity,
  float positions, and settlement.
- **Agency Field Team:** Physical inspections, onboarding visits, and
  routine supervision.
- **Compliance & AML Team:** Transaction monitoring, suspicious activity
  review, and regulatory reporting per the KYC & AML Compliance Policy.
- **Internal Audit:** Independent annual review of the agency channel.

## 6. Compliance & Enforcement

Non-compliance by Group employees is addressed per the Employee Code of
Conduct. Non-compliance by agents may result in fines per the Agency
Agreement, tier downgrade, suspension, or termination.

All material agency incidents must be reported per the Incident Response
Policy.
