# Data Protection & Privacy Policy

**Document ID:** TBP-POL-DP-001
**Version:** 2.4
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026
**Policy Owner:** Data Protection Officer (DPO)
**Approver:** Board Risk & Compliance Committee

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes how ThaBytePay collects, processes, stores, shares, and
disposes of personal data. It defines the rights of data subjects and the
obligations of the Group as a data controller and processor.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all personal data processed by ThaBytePay, regardless of
format (digital, paper, voice recordings) or location (on-premise, cloud,
third-party processors). It applies to data of customers, prospects, employees,
contractors, agents, vendors, and any other identifiable natural person.

## 3. Definitions

- **Personal Data:** Any information relating to an identified or identifiable
  natural person.
- **Sensitive Personal Data:** A subset of personal data including biometric
  data, health information, religious beliefs, and government identifiers.
- **Data Subject:** The natural person whose personal data is processed.
- **Data Controller:** The entity that determines the purposes and means of
  processing.
- **Data Processor:** Any third party that processes data on behalf of the
  Group.
- **Data Classification Tiers:** Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted.

## 4. Policy Statements

### 4.1 Lawful Basis for Processing

Personal data may only be processed where there is a lawful basis, including:

- Consent of the data subject
- Performance of a contract with the data subject
- Compliance with a legal obligation
- Protection of vital interests
- Legitimate interests of the Group (subject to balancing assessment)

### 4.2 Data Classification

All data must be classified into one of four tiers:

| Tier | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Public | No restriction on disclosure | Marketing brochures |
| Internal | Default for employee work product | Meeting minutes |
| Confidential | Disclosure could harm the Group | Strategic plans, salary information |
| Restricted | Disclosure could cause severe harm | Customer PINs, biometrics, account numbers |

Confidential and Restricted data must be encrypted at rest and in transit per
the Information Security Policy.

### 4.3 Customer Consent

- Customer consent for marketing communications must be **opt-in**. Pre-ticked
  boxes are not valid consent.
- Customers can withdraw consent at any time through the customer portal, USSD
  menu (option 9-4), or by visiting any branch.
- Consent records must be retained for the duration of the customer
  relationship plus 7 years.

### 4.4 Data Subject Rights

ThaBytePay honours the following rights, which can be exercised through
**dpo@thabytepay.com** or the customer portal:

- **Right of access:** Subjects may request a copy of their personal data.
  Responses are provided within 30 calendar days.
- **Right of rectification:** Inaccurate data must be corrected within 15
  business days of request.
- **Right of erasure:** Subjects may request deletion subject to legal
  retention requirements (e.g., AML records must be retained for 7 years).
- **Right of portability:** Subjects may request their data in a
  machine-readable format.
- **Right to object:** Subjects may object to processing for marketing,
  profiling, or automated decisioning.

### 4.5 Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|------------------|
| Active customer records | Duration of relationship + 7 years |
| Transaction records | 7 years from transaction date |
| KYC documents | 7 years from account closure |
| Marketing engagement logs | 24 months |
| Recruitment data (unsuccessful) | 12 months |
| Employee records (active) | Duration of employment |
| Employee records (terminated) | 7 years from termination |
| CCTV footage | 90 days |

Data exceeding retention periods must be securely destroyed per the Data
Destruction Standard Operating Procedure (TBP-SOP-DP-007).

### 4.6 Third-Party Data Sharing

Personal data may be shared with third parties only when:

- A Data Processing Agreement is signed
- Sharing is necessary for the agreed purpose
- The third party offers adequate data protection (verified by the DPO)
- The data subject has been informed or has consented as required

Cross-border transfers outside Kenya require explicit DPO approval and an
appropriate transfer mechanism.

### 4.7 Data Breach Notification

Suspected data breaches must be reported to the Data Protection Office within
**1 hour of discovery**. The DPO will:

- Convene a breach response team within 4 hours
- Notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours if
  there is a risk to data subjects
- Notify affected data subjects without undue delay if there is a high risk

See the Incident Response Policy for detailed handling procedures.

### 4.8 Privacy by Design

All new products, services, and systems must complete a Data Protection Impact
Assessment (DPIA) before launch. The DPO must sign off on DPIAs for any
processing involving sensitive personal data or large-scale processing.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Data Protection Officer (DPO):** Owns this policy, handles data subject
  requests, liaises with the regulator.
- **Data Owners:** Business unit heads with accountability for personal data
  in their function.
- **Data Stewards:** Day-to-day data quality and access management.
- **All Employees:** Must complete annual data protection training and report
  suspected breaches.

## 6. Compliance & Enforcement

Compliance is monitored through:

- Annual DPIA audit
- Quarterly review of data subject requests and response times
- Annual independent privacy audit
- Monitoring of regulator correspondence

Violations may result in disciplinary action up to termination and personal
liability for serious infringements.
