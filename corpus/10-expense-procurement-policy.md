# Expense & Procurement Policy

**Document ID:** TBP-POL-EP-001
**Version:** 3.0
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026
**Policy Owner:** Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
**Approver:** Executive Committee

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs how ThaBytePay employees commit Group funds — through
expense incurred for business purposes, procurement of goods and services,
and travel. It establishes authority levels, approval workflows, and
reimbursement rules.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors with delegated authority,
and any party authorised to spend on the Group's behalf.

## 3. Definitions

- **Procurement:** The process of acquiring goods or services from external
  vendors.
- **Expense:** A cost incurred by an employee in the course of business
  duties, eligible for reimbursement or already paid by the Group.
- **Approval Authority Matrix (AAM):** The schedule defining who can approve
  spending at each amount.

## 4. Policy Statements

### 4.1 Approval Authority Matrix

| Spending Tier | Limit per Item (KES) | Required Approval |
|---------------|----------------------|-------------------|
| Tier-A | Up to 50,000 | Line Manager |
| Tier-B | 50,001 to 250,000 | Department Head |
| Tier-C | 250,001 to 1,000,000 | Functional Director |
| Tier-D | 1,000,001 to 5,000,000 | Executive Committee |
| Tier-E | Above 5,000,000 | Board (Procurement Sub-Committee) |

Split purchasing — splitting a single requirement into smaller items to stay
under an approval threshold — is strictly prohibited.

### 4.2 Procurement Process

For purchases over 50,000 KES, the standard process is:

1. **Requisition** raised in the Procurement System with business
   justification.
2. **Approval** per the AAM.
3. **Vendor selection:**
   - Single vendor under 250,000 KES with approved supplier: direct.
   - 250,001 to 1,000,000 KES: minimum 3 quotations.
   - Above 1,000,000 KES: formal RFP/tender process.
4. **Contract signing** by an authorised signatory (CFO or delegate).
5. **Purchase order issued** to the vendor.
6. **Goods receipt** and **invoice approval** in the system.
7. **Payment** per agreed terms.

### 4.3 Vendor Onboarding

Before doing business with a new vendor:

- **Due diligence** including ownership disclosure and sanctions screening.
- **Tax compliance certificate** verification (current PIN, valid tax
  status).
- **Reference checks** for vendors above 1,000,000 KES annual value.
- **Information Security assessment** for vendors accessing Group systems
  or data (per Information Security Policy).
- **Data Processing Agreement** where personal data is processed.

### 4.4 Conflicts of Interest

Employees must declare any personal relationship or interest in a vendor
under consideration. Procurement officers may not be involved in selection
where they have a declared interest.

### 4.5 Travel and Entertainment

Travel must be:

- Pre-approved by Line Manager (domestic) or Department Head (international).
- Booked through the corporate travel desk where possible.
- Economy class for flights under 5 hours.
- Standard hotel category (3 to 4-star equivalent).

Daily allowances:

| Destination | Per Diem (KES equivalent) |
|-------------|---------------------------|
| Nairobi metro | 1,500 |
| Other Kenya cities | 3,500 |
| East Africa region | 7,500 |
| Other Africa | 12,000 |
| Outside Africa | 18,000 |

Entertainment of clients or partners requires Line Manager pre-approval. Any
gift or hospitality received over 5,000 KES must be declared per the
Employee Code of Conduct.

### 4.6 Expense Reimbursement

- Expense claims must be submitted via the Expense Management System within
  30 days of incurrence.
- Original receipts (or scanned copies for digital purchases) are required
  for any claim over 1,000 KES.
- Reimbursements are paid in the next payroll cycle following approval.
- Claims older than 60 days require Functional Director approval.

### 4.7 Corporate Cards

Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel frequently or have
delegated purchasing authority. Cardholders must:

- Use the card only for business expenses
- Submit a reconciliation each month with supporting receipts
- Report lost or stolen cards immediately per the Card Issuance & Management
  Policy

Personal use of corporate cards is a violation of this policy.

### 4.8 Audit Trail

All expense and procurement transactions are logged in the financial
system. Internal Audit reviews a sample annually.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities

- **CFO:** Owns this policy.
- **Procurement Team:** Vendor selection support, RFP management, contract
  drafting.
- **Finance Operations:** Invoice processing, expense reimbursement,
  reconciliation.
- **Department Heads:** Budget responsibility and approval per the AAM.
- **All Employees:** Comply with spending limits and document business
  justification.

## 6. Compliance & Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action and may also
require recovery of funds. Fraudulent claims will be treated as serious
misconduct per the Employee Code of Conduct.
