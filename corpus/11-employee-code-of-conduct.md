# Employee Code of Conduct

**Document ID:** TBP-POL-EC-001
**Version:** 4.0
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026
**Policy Owner:** Chief People Officer
**Approver:** Board

---

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct sets out the ethical standards ThaBytePay expects from
every employee. It is the foundation of trust between the Group, its
customers, regulators, and society.

## 2. Scope

This Code applies to all employees, contractors, interns, board members, and
any individual representing the Group in any capacity.

## 3. Definitions

- **Conflict of Interest:** Any personal interest that may improperly
  influence, or appear to influence, the employee's duties.
- **Whistleblowing:** Confidential reporting of suspected misconduct.
- **Material Information:** Information that could reasonably affect
  decisions of customers, regulators, or markets.

## 4. Policy Statements

### 4.1 Honesty and Integrity

Employees shall:

- Act honestly, in good faith, and in the best interests of the Group.
- Not misrepresent facts to customers, colleagues, regulators, or third
  parties.
- Not engage in fraud, bribery, or corruption in any form.
- Accurately record transactions, time, expenses, and any other matters
  within their responsibility.

### 4.2 Customer Treatment

Customers are at the heart of the Group. Employees must:

- Treat all customers with respect and without discrimination.
- Provide clear, accurate, and complete information about products and
  fees.
- Never recommend products that are unsuitable for the customer's needs.
- Resolve complaints promptly and fairly (see Customer Complaints Standard).

### 4.3 Conflicts of Interest

Employees must:

- Disclose any actual, potential, or perceived conflict of interest to
  their Line Manager and HR within 5 business days of becoming aware.
- Not approve transactions involving themselves, family members, or
  entities they have an interest in.
- Declare outside employment, directorships, or business interests at
  onboarding and on any change.

Examples requiring declaration:

- Family member employed by a vendor or competitor
- Personal investment in a customer or vendor entity
- Outside paid or unpaid employment

### 4.4 Gifts and Hospitality

- Employees may not solicit gifts, favours, or hospitality from customers,
  vendors, or anyone with whom the Group has or seeks a business
  relationship.
- Modest, infrequent gifts under 5,000 KES (e.g., calendars, branded
  trinkets) may be accepted without declaration.
- Gifts or hospitality above 5,000 KES must be declared in the Gifts
  Register within 5 business days and may be subject to return, decline,
  or donation per Line Manager direction.
- Cash, gift cards, or cash equivalents may never be accepted regardless
  of amount.

### 4.5 Confidentiality

Employees must:

- Protect Confidential and Restricted information per the Information
  Security and Data Protection Policies.
- Not use Confidential information for personal benefit or to benefit
  others.
- Maintain confidentiality obligations after employment ends.

### 4.6 Insider Information

Employees with access to material non-public information must not:

- Trade in any security based on that information.
- Tip off others who may trade.
- Use the information for any personal advantage.

### 4.7 Anti-Bribery and Corruption

The Group has zero tolerance for bribery and corruption. Employees must
not:

- Offer, give, request, or accept bribes in any form.
- Make facilitation payments to public officials.
- Use third parties to do indirectly what is prohibited directly.

Any suspected bribery must be reported immediately via the Whistleblowing
Channel.

### 4.8 Workplace Conduct

The Group is committed to a respectful, safe, and inclusive workplace.
Prohibited conduct includes:

- Harassment of any kind (sexual, racial, religious, disability-based)
- Bullying or intimidation
- Discrimination in hiring, evaluation, or promotion
- Substance abuse on Group premises or while on Group duties
- Violence or threats of violence

### 4.9 Use of Group Resources

Employees must use Group resources responsibly per the Acceptable Use
Policy. Group assets, time, and information are not for personal benefit.

### 4.10 External Communications

Public statements representing the Group (including media interviews and
social media posts that identify the speaker as a Group employee) must
follow the External Communications Standard. Personal opinions must be
clearly identified as personal.

### 4.11 Whistleblowing

The Group encourages reporting of suspected misconduct. Reports can be
made:

- To Line Manager (where appropriate)
- To HR Business Partner
- Via the confidential Whistleblowing Channel (whistleblowing@thabytepay.com
  or 0800-SPEAKUP)
- To the Chair of the Audit Committee for matters involving senior
  leadership

Retaliation against whistleblowers is strictly prohibited and is itself a
serious violation of this Code.

### 4.12 Compliance with Laws and Regulations

Employees must comply with all applicable laws, including:

- Banking and financial services regulations
- Data protection and privacy laws
- Anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing laws
- Tax obligations
- Employment law

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Board:** Sets the tone from the top and approves this Code.
- **Executive Committee:** Champions the Code and ensures consistent
  application.
- **Line Managers:** Model the Code, address concerns, and escalate as
  needed.
- **Chief People Officer:** Owns this Code and the disciplinary framework.
- **Internal Audit and Compliance:** Independent oversight.
- **All Employees:** Read, understand, and comply with this Code. Sign an
  acknowledgement at onboarding and annually.

## 6. Compliance & Enforcement

Violations are investigated by HR with support from Compliance and
Internal Audit as appropriate. Disciplinary actions range from formal
warnings to summary dismissal, depending on severity. Criminal conduct is
referred to law enforcement.
