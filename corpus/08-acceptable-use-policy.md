# Acceptable Use Policy

**Document ID:** TBP-POL-AU-001
**Version:** 2.1
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026
**Policy Owner:** Chief Information Officer (CIO)
**Approver:** Executive Committee

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines acceptable use of ThaBytePay's information systems,
network resources, and Group-issued devices. It exists to protect employees,
customers, and the Group from accidental, negligent, or deliberate misuse.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, interns, and any third
party using Group systems or devices.

## 3. Definitions

- **Group Resources:** Any IT system, network, application, device, or
  communication service provided by the Group.
- **Personal Use:** Use of Group resources for purposes unrelated to job
  duties.

## 4. Policy Statements

### 4.1 General Principles

Employees are expected to use Group resources:

- For legitimate business purposes
- In compliance with all applicable laws and Group policies
- Responsibly, treating equipment with care
- With awareness that there is no expectation of privacy on Group systems

### 4.2 Permitted Personal Use

Limited personal use is permitted provided it:

- Does not interfere with job performance
- Does not consume disproportionate resources
- Does not violate any Group policy
- Is not for personal financial gain

### 4.3 Prohibited Activities

The following are strictly prohibited:

- Accessing, downloading, or transmitting pornography, hate speech, or
  illegal content
- Using Group resources to harass, threaten, or intimidate anyone
- Attempting to bypass security controls
- Sharing Group accounts or credentials with anyone, including colleagues
- Installing unauthorised software
- Connecting unapproved devices to the corporate network
- Mining or trading cryptocurrencies on Group infrastructure
- Engaging in any activity that violates the Information Security Policy

### 4.4 Email and Messaging

- Group email is for business and limited personal use only.
- Confidential or Restricted data (per the Data Protection & Privacy Policy)
  must not be sent to external recipients without prior approval and
  encryption.
- Auto-forwarding to personal email accounts is prohibited.
- Phishing simulations are conducted regularly; clicks are logged and
  trigger micro-training.

### 4.5 Internet Use

- Web browsing is filtered by the Group's secure web gateway.
- Categories blocked by default: gambling, adult content, malware sites,
  unsanctioned cloud storage, peer-to-peer file sharing.
- Requests to unblock business-justified sites can be made through the IT
  Service Desk.

### 4.6 Mobile Devices

- Group-issued mobile devices are subject to Mobile Device Management (MDM)
  enrolment, which enables remote wipe, encryption enforcement, and
  application controls.
- Personal devices accessing Group email or systems must enrol in MDM (BYOD
  programme) or be denied access.
- Jailbroken or rooted devices are prohibited.

### 4.7 Removable Media

- USB drives, external hard drives, and similar removable media may be used
  only if approved and encrypted.
- Confidential or Restricted data transfers to removable media require DPO
  approval.

### 4.8 Social Media

- Public statements about ThaBytePay must comply with the External
  Communications Standard.
- Employees must not disclose Confidential or Restricted information on
  social media.
- Customer information must never be referenced on social media, even
  anonymously.

### 4.9 Monitoring

- Group resources are subject to monitoring for security, compliance, and
  operational reasons.
- Employees consent to monitoring as a condition of resource access.
- Monitoring is conducted proportionately and in compliance with the Data
  Protection & Privacy Policy.

### 4.10 Reporting

Employees must report:

- Suspected policy violations (their own or others')
- Lost or stolen devices (within 1 hour, per Information Security Policy)
- Suspected phishing or fraud attempts
- Unauthorised access attempts

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities

- **CIO:** Owns this policy.
- **IT Service Desk:** Day-to-day enforcement, exceptions, support.
- **Information Security:** Investigation of suspected violations.
- **HR Business Partners:** Disciplinary process coordination.

## 6. Compliance & Enforcement

Violations may result in disciplinary action up to termination per the
Employee Code of Conduct. Activities that constitute criminal offences will
be reported to law enforcement.
