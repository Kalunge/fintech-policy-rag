# KYC & AML Compliance Policy

**Document ID:** TBP-POL-KA-001
**Version:** 5.0
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026
**Policy Owner:** Chief Compliance Officer
**Approver:** Board Risk & Compliance Committee

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out ThaBytePay's framework for Know Your Customer (KYC),
Customer Due Diligence (CDD), Anti-Money Laundering (AML), Counter-Financing
of Terrorism (CFT), and sanctions compliance. It is the cornerstone of the
Group's financial crime risk management.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all customer relationships, all transactions, and all
employees and agents involved in onboarding, transaction monitoring, or
relationship management.

## 3. Definitions

- **KYC:** Know Your Customer — verification of customer identity.
- **CDD:** Customer Due Diligence — risk-based assessment of the customer
  relationship.
- **EDD:** Enhanced Due Diligence — heightened scrutiny for higher-risk
  customers.
- **PEP:** Politically Exposed Person.
- **STR:** Suspicious Transaction Report.
- **UBO:** Ultimate Beneficial Owner.
- **Sanctions Lists:** UN, EU, OFAC, and Kenyan-domestic lists screened by
  the Group.

## 4. Policy Statements

### 4.1 KYC Tiers

ThaBytePay applies a tiered KYC model:

| Tier | Verification | Account Limits |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| Tier-1 (Basic) | Mobile number + ID number cross-check | Balance: 50,000 KES / Daily TX: 25,000 KES |
| Tier-2 (Standard) | Government ID + proof of address + photo capture | Balance: 1,000,000 KES / Daily TX: 200,000 KES |
| Tier-3 (Enhanced) | Tier-2 + source of funds + occupation verification | No standard limit (subject to monitoring) |
| Corporate | Business registration + UBO disclosure + director KYC | Custom per relationship agreement |

### 4.2 Customer Onboarding

Onboarding steps:

1. **Identity collection:** Full name, date of birth, government ID number,
   nationality, address, phone number, email.
2. **Document capture:** Photo of ID and customer selfie (liveness checked).
3. **Sanctions and PEP screening:** Automated screening against all
   configured lists. Hits trigger manual review by Compliance.
4. **Risk scoring:** The Onboarding Risk Engine assigns a score 0-100 based on
   geography, occupation, expected activity, and product type.
5. **Tier assignment:** Based on documents collected and risk score.

Onboarding decisions are issued in real time for Tier-1 and Tier-2, within
2 business days for Tier-3, and within 5 business days for Corporate.

### 4.3 Enhanced Due Diligence

EDD is applied to:

- Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) and their close associates
- High-risk geographies (per the Group's risk-rated country list)
- Customers with cash-intensive businesses
- Customers requesting cross-border services
- Any customer flagged by transaction monitoring

EDD includes source of wealth documentation, senior management approval, and
relationship review at least annually.

### 4.4 Ongoing Monitoring

- All transactions are monitored in real time by the Transaction Monitoring
  System.
- Alert thresholds are configured based on customer risk profile and product
  type.
- Alerts are triaged by the AML Operations team within 4 business hours.
- Confirmed suspicions are escalated to the Money Laundering Reporting
  Officer (MLRO).

### 4.5 Sanctions Screening

- All customers are screened against sanctions lists at onboarding and
  rescreened daily.
- All international transactions are screened in real time before processing.
- Sanctions matches block the transaction pending Compliance review.
- The MLRO is responsible for determining true matches and reporting to the
  relevant authorities.

### 4.6 Suspicious Transaction Reporting

- Any employee or agent who suspects money laundering, terrorism financing,
  or other financial crime must escalate to Compliance via the internal
  reporting tool.
- The MLRO assesses the report and, where appropriate, files an STR with
  the Financial Reporting Centre within the statutory deadline.
- "Tipping off" the customer is strictly prohibited.

### 4.7 Record Keeping

- KYC documents and CDD records: retained for 7 years from account closure.
- Transaction records: retained for 7 years from transaction date.
- STR records: retained for 7 years from filing.

### 4.8 Training

- All employees receive AML/CFT training upon joining and annually
  thereafter.
- Customer-facing staff receive role-specific training every 6 months.
- Training completion is recorded; non-completion blocks system access.

### 4.9 Periodic Review

Customer relationships are reviewed periodically based on risk tier:

| Risk Tier | Review Frequency |
|-----------|------------------|
| Low | Every 36 months |
| Medium | Every 18 months |
| High | Every 12 months |
| PEP / Highest Risk | Every 6 months |

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Chief Compliance Officer:** Owns this policy and the financial crime
  programme.
- **Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO):** Responsible for STR filing
  and regulator liaison.
- **AML Operations:** Day-to-day alert triage and customer outreach.
- **Branch and Agent Network:** Front-line KYC collection and verification.
- **All Employees:** Must complete training, report suspicions, and never
  tip off customers.

## 6. Compliance & Enforcement

Non-compliance with this policy may result in disciplinary action and may
also constitute a criminal offence under applicable AML/CFT laws.

The programme is subject to:

- Internal audit at least annually
- Independent external review every 24 months
- Regulator inspections as scheduled
