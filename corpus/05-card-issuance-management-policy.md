# Card Issuance & Management Policy

**Document ID:** TBP-POL-CI-001
**Version:** 2.3
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2026
**Policy Owner:** Head of Cards & Digital Payments
**Approver:** Chief Operations Officer

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the issuance, activation, ongoing management, and
termination of payment cards offered by ThaBytePay, including debit, credit,
prepaid, and virtual cards. It ensures cardholder data security, regulatory
compliance, and consistent customer experience.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees involved in card production, issuance,
servicing, and termination, and to all systems handling cardholder data.

## 3. Definitions

- **PAN:** Primary Account Number (the 16-digit card number).
- **CVV/CVC:** Card Verification Value/Code.
- **EMV:** Europay, Mastercard, and Visa chip card standard.
- **3DS:** 3-D Secure (Verified by Visa, Mastercard SecureCode, etc.).
- **Card Lifecycle:** Issuance → activation → in-use → expiry/loss → reissue
  → termination.

## 4. Policy Statements

### 4.1 Card Products

ThaBytePay issues the following card products:

| Product | Type | Schemes | Daily Limits |
|---------|------|---------|--------------|
| Classic Debit | Debit | Visa, Mastercard | ATM: 40,000 KES / POS: 100,000 KES |
| Premier Debit | Debit | Visa, Mastercard | ATM: 100,000 KES / POS: 500,000 KES |
| Standard Credit | Credit | Mastercard | Based on credit limit |
| Prepaid Travel | Prepaid | Visa | Customer-loaded balance |
| Virtual Card | Prepaid | Mastercard | Customer-loaded balance |

### 4.2 Eligibility and Issuance

- Card issuance is restricted to customers with completed KYC per the KYC &
  AML Compliance Policy.
- Credit cards additionally require credit assessment and approval by the
  Credit Risk team.
- Customer requests can be made via branch, agent, mobile app, or USSD.
- Cards are personalised and produced at the Group's certified card
  production facility, which is PCI Card Production certified.
- Standard issuance turnaround: 5 business days from approval. Express
  issuance (24 hours) is available for an additional fee.

### 4.3 PIN Management

- PINs are generated by the issuing system and are never known to any
  employee.
- PIN mailers are printed at a separate time and location from card
  production, then delivered separately to the customer.
- Customers may set or change PINs at any Group ATM, via the mobile app
  (with biometric authentication), or at a branch.
- After 3 incorrect PIN attempts, the card is blocked. Unblock requires
  customer verification through Customer Care.

### 4.4 Activation

- New cards arrive in an inactive state.
- Customers must activate the card via:
  - Mobile app (with login + OTP)
  - Branch in-person with valid ID
  - USSD menu (option 4-1) with last 4 digits + DOB
  - First successful ATM PIN change
- Activation deadline: 90 days from production. Unactivated cards are
  destroyed and customer notified.

### 4.5 Loss, Theft, and Card Blocking

- Customers must report loss or theft immediately via the 24/7 Card Hotline
  (0800-CARDS), mobile app, or any branch.
- Card hotlisting must be effected within 5 minutes of report.
- Replacement card issuance must be initiated within 24 business hours and
  delivered within 5 business days (or 24 hours for express, fee applies).
- Liability for fraudulent transactions before report is capped per the
  customer terms and applicable consumer protection laws.

### 4.6 Card Data Security

- Full PANs may only be displayed to authorised employees with a documented
  business need; access is logged.
- PANs displayed on screens or printed must be masked (first 6 + last 4
  digits visible).
- CVV/CVC values must never be stored in any Group system.
- All systems handling cardholder data must comply with the Information
  Security Policy and be in scope of the annual PCI DSS audit.

### 4.7 Transaction Authentication

- All e-commerce transactions must be authenticated via 3DS where supported
  by the merchant.
- Card-not-present transactions exceeding 10,000 KES must require 3DS by
  default; exemptions are subject to the Group's exemption policy.
- High-value POS transactions (above 50,000 KES) prompt for PIN entry
  regardless of card scheme floor limits.

### 4.8 Renewals and Expiry

- Standard card validity: 36 months.
- Renewal cards are produced automatically 60 days before expiry for
  customers in good standing.
- Customers may opt out of automatic renewal at any time.
- Expired cards must be cut through the magnetic stripe and chip and
  disposed of securely.

### 4.9 Card Termination

A card is terminated when:

- The customer requests termination
- The account is closed
- The card is reported lost or stolen and not replaced
- The card is identified as compromised (per Information Security findings)
- Fraud activity is confirmed

Terminated cards are recorded in the central blacklist and may not be
reactivated.

### 4.10 Disputes and Chargebacks

- Customers may dispute transactions within 120 days of the transaction date.
- The Card Disputes team must acknowledge disputes within 1 business day and
  resolve within 30 calendar days (or 60 days for cross-border).
- Chargeback decisions follow the issuing scheme rulebook.

## 5. Roles & Responsibilities

- **Head of Cards & Digital Payments:** Owns this policy and the card
  portfolio.
- **Card Operations:** Day-to-day issuance, activation, and servicing.
- **Card Production Facility:** Personalisation, PIN mailing, dispatch.
- **Card Disputes Team:** Chargeback processing and customer resolution.
- **Fraud Operations:** Real-time monitoring and case handling.

## 6. Compliance & Enforcement

The card programme is subject to:

- Annual PCI DSS audit
- Card scheme compliance audits (Visa, Mastercard)
- Internal audit at least annually
- Regulator inspections as required
